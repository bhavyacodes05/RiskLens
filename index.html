Scenario assumptions

How are bonds shocked?

Bond P&L ≈ −V × (KRD₂ × Δy₂ + KRD₅ × Δy₅ + KRD₁₀ × Δy₁₀). Key-rate durations sum to total modified duration. This first-order model supports nonparallel moves; it omits convexity and cross-gamma. The supplied sensitivities are illustrative, not calibrated to real bonds.

Do exceptions arrive together?

How uncertain are these estimates?

Wilson assumes independent exceptions. The block bootstrap resamples runs of 10 observations and can reflect short-range dependence. Sparse tails, regime changes and the chosen block length limit both approaches. VaR/ES intervals describe estimation uncertainty, not next-day loss ranges.

Interpretation and limitations

Red dots mark loss > the preceding window's VaR. The Kupiec test checks unconditional frequency. The Christoffersen independence test uses 00/01/10/11 transitions to assess first-order dependence; conditional coverage combines the two likelihood ratios. Its p-value uses a chi-square approximation, which can be weak with few exceptions. Synthetic data and a fixed portfolio cannot establish real-world model performance.

Source and conventions

Reproducibility and audit trail

Save audit run records inputs' SHA-256, model version, UTC timestamp, confidence, exposure scale, shocks and results in SQLite plus a JSON report. SQL example: SELECT id, timestamp_utc, confidence, scale FROM runs;

Model boundaries

Educational implementation. Positions, sensitivities, scenarios and limits are illustrative. Observations come from the selected labelled dataset. No Nomura affiliation or proprietary data. One equity factor, three Treasury par-yield nodes, one currency; no security-specific basis risk, funding, liquidity, counterparty credit, derivatives, or regulatory capital calculation. Scenario losses have no assigned probability and must not be added to 1-day VaR.

Before you call it a good hedge

A hedge can reduce one source of risk while leaving others unchanged. Compare the custom scenario P&L, daily VaR and gross exposure together. A lower VaR alone does not settle the decision.
